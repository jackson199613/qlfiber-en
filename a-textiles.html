  • Conductive materials are hard to run on standard sewing and knitting machines.
  • Multiple resistance grades are needed to match different battery designs.

How we work together

  • Specified by resistance, wire diameter, ply count and insulation material; small-lot trial sewing is supported.

What buyers verify

  • Resistance tolerance and heating uniformity.
  • Resistance retention after wash and flex cycles.
  • Machine efficiency in sewing and weaving.
Heated jackets, gloves and blankets fail when the heating element drifts, breaks or overheats. QL's stainless steel fiber yarns and insulated heating wires deliver stable resistance (1–64 Ω/m options), even heat and long flex and wash life — and they run on standard sewing and weaving equipment. For workwear, our blended yarns provide anti-static and shielding performance that lasts through washing. Tell us your voltage and heating layout and we will recommend a resistance and send samples.
