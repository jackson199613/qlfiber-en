03  ·  TECHNICAL TEXTILES & SLEEVES

Woven, knitted and braided metal — for heat that destroys fabric

Elastic knitted sleeves, braided sleeves, woven cloth, tape and rope made from 316L metal fiber, rated 350 °C continuous and 1100 °C short term, with no coating to shed.

OverviewMetal Fiber & YarnSintered Felt & MediaTechnical TextilesFilter ElementsOil Recovery Systems

Metal fiber is soft enough to be knitted, braided and woven on textile machinery, so it can take the shape a part actually needs: a sleeve that grips a roller, a tape that wraps a toothed bar, a cloth that covers a bending mould. Finished goods are heat-set so that, under normal cutting and handling, they do not fuzz or shed.

Where it is used

  • Ceramic roller protection in glass tempering furnaces
  • Toothed bars and rack protection in chemical strengthening
  • Hot bending mould covers for automotive glass
  • Thermocouple and cable sleeving
  • Suction cup and clamp cushioning, container pusher bars
  • Equipment earthing and static elimination
