03  ·  TECHNICAL TEXTILES & SLEEVES

Heat-resistant cushioning materials for glass manufacturing

Rope, felt, thread, elastic sleeves, braided sleeves and tape or cloth made from 316L metal fiber, rated 350–1100 °C — protection and cushioning for the hot side of glass production.

Products

Products in this line

High-temperature metal fiber rope

High-strength rope, rated 350–1100 °C, abrasion and corrosion resistant with a smooth surface. Used to cover automotive glass mould frames and container-glass pusher bars (L-frames), and as anti-static or high-temperature sealing rope. Φ1–12 mm, square or round.

Ask about this product

High-temperature metal fiber felt

Three-dimensional porous cushioning material, rated 350–1100 °C, that damps vibration and absorbs impact. Commonly used as heat-resistant pads for glass suction cups and clamps. 2–3 mm thick.

Ask about this product

High-temperature metal fiber thread

Flexible, conductive thread rated 350–1100 °C and resistant to acid, alkali and salt. Used to wind chemical-tempering racks, as functional thread in specialty clothing and in high-performance wiring.

Ask about this product

High-temperature elastic metal fiber sleeve

Elastic tubular protection rated 350–1100 °C — keeps its shape, does not crack or powder at end of life. Typical use: ceramic roller sleeves in physical tempering furnaces. TGZ series.

Ask about this product

High-temperature braided metal fiber sleeve

Braided tubular protection rated 350–1100 °C, resistant to potassium nitrate, sulphuric acid and dilute hydrochloric acid, with lasting EMI shielding. Typical use: rack protection in chemical tempering furnaces. TGBX series.

Ask about this product

High-temperature metal fiber tape / cloth

Flat heat-resistant material, rated 350–1100 °C, smooth, breathable and drapable. Used as mould-covering tape and mould cloth in glass forming, and for wiping and cushioning hot parts. 0.35–0.5 mm thick; widths 1000 / 1200 / 1500 mm.

Ask about this product

Metal fiber is soft enough to be knitted, braided and woven on textile machinery, so it can take the shape a part actually needs: a sleeve that grips a ceramic roller, a braided sleeve over a chemical-tempering rack, a cloth that covers a forming mould, a felt pad under a suction cup. All six products share the same core properties: rated 350–1100 °C, excellent oxidation resistance in hot air, strong, abrasion and shear resistant, with a smooth, burr-free surface.

Where it is used

  • Ceramic roller sleeves in physical tempering furnaces
  • Rack protection in chemical tempering furnaces
  • Mould-covering tape and mould cloth in glass forming
  • Cushioning pads for glass suction cups and clamps
  • Covers for automotive glass mould frames and container-glass pusher bars
  • Gas turbines, cables, high-temperature equipment and EMI shielding
