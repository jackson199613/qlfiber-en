Milestones

2002 to today

2002

Founded

The company is established in Longyan, Fujian, to produce metal fiber by bundle drawing.

2006

Sintered felt

Vacuum sintering of multi-layer fiber webs begins, opening the filtration media business.

2010

Filter elements

Element assembly is brought in house — pleating, support mesh lamination, end fittings and welding.

2013

Textiles for glass

Knitted and braided sleeves for glass tempering rollers enter series production.

2016

Conductive yarn

Blended and insulated conductive yarn for heating and shielding applications is industrialised.

2019

Research partnerships

Provincial research on metal fiber forming; collaboration on electromagnetic shielding fabric with the Ningbo Institute of Materials Technology, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

2020

Oil recovery

The coalescing oil recovery system is commercialised for nickel-cobalt extraction circuits.

2021

NEEQ listing

The company lists on the National Equities Exchange and Quotations under code 872336.

2023

Smart wearables

A provincial project on flexible functional materials for smart wearables begins.

2024

Scale

The largest coalescing oil recovery system to date, at 600 m³/h, enters service.

2026

Equipment recognition

High-efficiency metal coalescing oil removal equipment is taken up as a provincial project and recognised as first-of-its-kind major technical equipment.
