Safety Month 2026: everyone trained, everyone ready

During national Safety Production Month we ran plant-wide hazard checks, training and live drills for firefighting, evacuation and first aid.

Source: QL Metal Fiber official WeChat account (in Chinese)

Safety comes first. To strengthen safety awareness and accountability across the company, we ran a series of activities for national Safety Production Month 2026.

Under the theme “everyone talks safety, everyone can respond”, and with the specific risks of new-materials production in mind, we held a kick-off meeting and safety briefings that reached every employee.

We carried out a plant-wide hazard inspection focused on high-temperature equipment, electrical systems, fire protection and shop-floor work areas, logging every finding and closing it out. We also put summer heat-protection measures in place for front-line staff.

Practical drills covered firefighting, emergency evacuation, and first aid for electric shock and mechanical injury, so that employees can respond to real incidents and help one another.

Safety does not end in June. We will keep hazard checks, training and drills running all year round.
