QL New Materials Innovation Research Institute opens a Xiamen office

The research institute's new office in the Xiamen–Longyan Mountain-Sea Cooperation Science and Technology Park is now open, extending our “research in Xiamen, production in Longyan” model.

Source: QL Metal Fiber official WeChat account (in Chinese) · View original

The Xiamen office of the QL New Materials Innovation Research Institute has moved into the Xiamen–Longyan Mountain-Sea Cooperation Science and Technology Park (Unit 01, 2nd floor). It puts our research team closer to Xiamen's talent pool and innovation ecosystem, while manufacturing stays in Longyan.

Fitted out and in use

The office has been fully fitted out and is in use. Research facilities will be added over time, building a base for technology development, online sales and industry collaboration.

Who we are looking for

Alongside research on metal fiber technology, the office is building an e-commerce and online marketing team to widen the market for our materials. We are inviting:

R&D: senior development engineers, materials specialists and project partners.

E-commerce: cross-border and domestic e-commerce operations and online marketing staff.

Industry partners: universities, research institutes, technology incubators and supply-chain partners.

Site management: office and team management staff, and R&D and design managers.
