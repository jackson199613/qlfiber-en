Intellectual property matters to a buyer for one practical reason: it shows the supplier owns the process rather than copying it, and that the specification you qualify will still exist in five years.

R&D OverviewLab & TestingPatents & Standards

Our patent portfolio covers 62 granted and pending patents, 16 of which are invention patents. They cluster in four areas: fiber forming and bundle drawing, graded sintered structures, surface-modified coalescing media, and the equipment built around them.

Our chairman is the lead drafter of national standards for metal fiber sintered felt and serves as deputy secretary-general of the national screening and sieving standardisation committee, and as a filtration and separation expert with the Chemical Industry and Engineering Society of China. Standards work is slow and unglamorous, but it is the reason our test reports read the same way a buyer's own laboratory would write them.

Patent and standard numbers can be supplied on request for a qualification file. Some documents are in Chinese only; we will provide a certified English summary where one is needed.
