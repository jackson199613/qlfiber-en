More Applications · Co-Development

Your conditions, our fiber.

Metal fiber is a platform material, not a single product. If your process needs the heat, corrosion and conductivity of metal together with the flexibility and porosity of a fiber, the application does not have to be on this list. Tell us the conditions and we will propose the fiber, the form and the alloy, build samples in our lab and work with you through small-batch validation to stable supply.

Directions already in service include regenerative heat-exchanger media, high-temperature silencer and sound-absorbing material, resin grinding wheels, high-temperature conveyor belts and seat moulds, fluidised-bed gas distribution plates, membrane supports, catalyst carriers, vacuum-pump protection filtration, pipeline freeze-protection heating, heating elements for medical devices and robot cabling.
