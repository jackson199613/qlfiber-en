20KIA3 sintered metal fiber felt

A 316L depth-filtration medium rated 20 µm absolute at 80% porosity and 0.45 mm thick — the middle of the KIA3 series, and the grade most often chosen as a starting point for polymer melt and high-temperature gas duty.

Where it is used

Typical duty

  • Polymer melt filtration in chemical fiber and film lines
  • Viscose dope filtration
  • High-temperature gas filtration and dust removal
  • Pre-filtration ahead of membranes and vacuum pump protection
  • Hydraulic and fuel filtration in a pleated element

Selecting within the series

Moving finer raises bubble point and lowers permeability; moving coarser does the reverse. If the rating has to stay at 20 µm but the interval between changes is too short, the thickened 20KIA3K at 1.1 mm adds depth without changing the rating.

Send the medium, temperature, pressure drop, flow and solids load and we will confirm whether this grade is the right starting point — or propose the one that is.
Questions

About this grade

Can this grade be cleaned and reused?

Yes. The felt is metallurgically bonded at every fiber contact, so back-flushing, ultrasonic and chemical cleaning all return it to service.

What changes if my differential pressure rises too quickly?

Usually thickness rather than rating. The thickened 20KIA3K at 1.1 mm holds the same 20 µm rating with greater dirt-holding capacity.

Can you supply it as a finished element?

Yes. We make the media, the support mesh assembly and the element, so rating, strength and end fittings are specified together.

Are these values guaranteed?

They are typical values, confirmed on the inspection report for each lot. They apply to the stated test conditions and are a basis for selection, not a performance warranty.
