What we commit to

Consistency, and evidence of it

Buyers rarely fail a metal fiber product on its first lot. They fail it on the fourth, when something has drifted. Our answer is to measure the same things in the same way on every lot, issue the report with the shipment, and keep a retained sample so a later lot can be compared against the one you approved.

Where your qualification protocol requires a test we do not hold in house, we will say so plainly and arrange a third-party laboratory rather than substitute a different method.

Performance values published on this site apply to the stated test conditions. They are a basis for selection and comparison, not a warranty for your process. Final suitability is established by a trial under your own conditions.
