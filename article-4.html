Largest coalescing oil recovery unit to date enters service at 600 m³/h

A single-train coalescing oil recovery system rated 600 m³/h is now operating on a nickel-cobalt extraction circuit, the largest capacity we have supplied in one unit.

The unit is a trough-type configuration, selected because floor space on the plant was limited and an existing clarifier trough could be used as the basis for the installation.

Why trough type at this scale

Tank-type systems are skid-mountable and suit flows up to about 30 m³/h. Above that, a trough configuration keeps the footprint compact — roughly one third to two thirds of the hourly throughput figure — and allows retrofit into an existing clarifier rather than a new civil structure.

The system is purely physical: no demulsifier is added and no chemical sludge is created. Recovered organic phase returns to the circuit, and the load on downstream activated carbon and resin falls accordingly.

Our installed base in this application includes leading nickel-cobalt hydrometallurgy producers in China. Customer names are not published without written consent.
