QL Metal Fiber at FILTECH: what buyers asked about

Three questions came up repeatedly at the show: cleanability, lot-to-lot consistency, and whether elements can be made to fit existing housings.

Trade shows are a good sample of what the market is actually worried about. Three questions dominated our conversations.

Can it really be cleaned?

Yes — the structure is all metal and metallurgically bonded at every fiber contact, so back-flushing, ultrasonic and chemical cleaning all return it to service. This is the main economic difference from disposable media, and it is why the comparison should be made on cost per operating hour rather than on unit price.

How consistent is it between lots?

Every lot is measured for bubble point, permeability, efficiency, porosity, thickness and weight, and ships with an inspection report. We keep a retained sample so a later lot can be compared against the one you approved.

Will it fit our housings?

Pleated cartridges run Φ35 to 60 mm and 425 to 1250 mm long with end fittings made to your drawing. Because we make the media, the support mesh assembly and the element, rating, strength and fit are specified together rather than negotiated between suppliers.

Values quoted apply to the stated test or operating conditions and are a basis for selection, not a performance warranty. Final suitability is established by a trial under your own conditions.
