Third prize at the Fujian Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition

Our metal fiber and functional filter media project took third prize in the provincial final, presenting a coalescing oil–water separation package for reuse-grade effluent.

Source: QL Metal Fiber official WeChat account (in Chinese) · View original

The final of the Fujian Innovation and Entrepreneurship Competition has concluded. Fujian QL Metal Fiber's project on industrialising metal fiber and specialty functional filter media came through municipal selection, the provincial semi-final and the final, and took third prize among several hundred projects from across the province.

The project presented our new-generation coalescing oil–water separation package. Surface modification of the metal fiber and multi-layer graded coupling separate oil droplets down to 500 nm, with 99.5% oil removal and an effluent quality good enough to reuse in production.

A two-system integrated layout reduces equipment volume by 60%, and modular design makes retrofits in existing plants quick. Consumable cost falls by about 90%, recovered oil can be reused, payback is around one year, and the system can run unattended under PLC control.

The aim is to turn wastewater treatment from a cost centre into a source of recovered value.

At the final, our team presented the technical principle, process innovation, industrialisation and market outlook, and answered the judges' questions on technical barriers, import substitution and industry development.

QL Metal Fiber is a national “Little Giant” specialised enterprise and a National High-Tech Enterprise with more than twenty years in metal fiber. We run the full chain from fiber drawing and spinning to technical textiles and filter elements, producing micron-scale fiber at stable volume for fine filtration, high-temperature protection, anti-static and conductive applications, new energy, petrochemicals and glass manufacturing.

We will keep investing in metal fiber development and in bringing new results into production.
