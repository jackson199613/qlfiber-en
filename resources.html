Questions buyers actually ask

Materials

Which alloys can you supply as fiber?

316L, 304 and 302 are standard. FeCrAl, 904L, nickel-based alloys, Hastelloy, Monel, duplex alloys, copper and titanium are available, usually with a minimum quantity. Tell us the medium and temperature and we will propose the alloy rather than ask you to choose one.

How fine can the fiber be?

2 µm monofilament is the finest we draw. 12 µm is the usual diameter for textile and conductive applications, because it balances conductivity against the hand and strength of the yarn.

Is the metal plated on, or is the fiber solid metal?

Solid metal. The fiber is drawn from wire, so conductivity and corrosion resistance do not wash out, flex off or degrade with time, as they do with metallised or plated fibers.

What temperature can the material take?

Depends on the alloy and the form. 316L textiles are rated 350 °C continuous with short-term peaks to 1100 °C; sintered felt is generally used to 480 °C in the process medium; high-temperature filter bags are rated 350–1000 °C. Always state both normal and peak temperature at enquiry.

Filtration

What does “absolute rating” mean for your felt?

It is the largest particle that passes under the stated test. Our standard KIA3 series runs 10 to 60 µm absolute at 72 to 86% porosity. Bubble point rises as the rating gets finer and air permeability falls — that trade-off is the main thing to balance at selection.

Can the media be cleaned and reused?

Yes. The structure is all metal and metallurgically bonded at every fiber contact, so it can be back-flushed, ultrasonically cleaned or chemically cleaned and returned to service. That is the main economic difference from a disposable medium.

My differential pressure rises too quickly. What changes?

Usually thickness or structure rather than rating. The thickened KIA3K series at 1.1 mm raises dirt-holding capacity and flow at the same absolute rating. Send us the current element, the rating, the flow and the change-out interval.

Can you make elements to fit our existing housings?

Yes. Pleated cartridges run Φ35–60 mm and 425–1250 mm long, with end fittings made to your drawing. We make the media, the support mesh assembly and the element, so rating, strength and fit are specified together.

Textiles and conductive products

Do your sleeves shed fibers?

Finished goods are heat-set so that under normal cutting and handling they do not fuzz or shed. Sharp cut edges and heavily abrasive duty still benefit from edge treatment — describe the installation and we will advise.

What resistance can you hold on a conductive thread?

1 to 64 Ω/m, set by ply count and core count. Fiber resistance at room temperature varies with the state of the stainless steel fiber, so if your product has a resistance limit, state it at enquiry and we will test samples before qualification.

Can you blend metal fiber with our own yarn?

Yes. Blend ratios run from 0.5 to 100% stainless steel with aramid, polyester, PPS or cotton. We can trial-spin and trial-weave small quantities before you commit.

Working with us

Is there a minimum order?

Most technical textile products have no minimum order — a trial can be a single length. Special alloys and custom constructions carry a minimum, which we state with the quotation.

How fast are samples?

Stock samples ship in one to three days, freight normally collect. Made-to-order samples follow the development schedule agreed for the project.

What do you need from us to quote?

Medium and its chemistry; temperature in service and at peak; pressure and differential pressure; flow; particle size and solids load; cleaning or change-out regime; dimensions or a drawing. The more of these you give, the fewer rounds it takes.

Do you supply through distributors?

Both direct from the factory and through distributors. Tell us your market and volume and we will say which route fits.

Tell us your conditions

Send us your operating conditions, and we will send samples.

Temperature, medium, pressure, filtration rating, resistance or porosity — give us the numbers you work with and our engineers will recommend a configuration, quote it and arrange samples for your own testing.
